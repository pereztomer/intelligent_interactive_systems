# Project Summary

## Problem

Effective presentation skills are crucial for academic and professional success, yet individuals often lack access to expert feedback when practicing. Traditional practice methods—rehearsing alone or with non-expert peers—provide limited actionable insights. While existing tools may measure basic metrics like speaking pace, they fail to provide contextual, interpretable feedback that helps users understand *why* issues matter and *how* to improve. This gap leaves presenters without the expert-level coaching needed to refine their delivery, manage nervousness, and optimize their communication effectiveness.

## Solution

We present the **Presentation Rehearsal Coach**, an intelligent system that provides expert-level feedback on presentation skills through AI-powered analysis. The system allows users to practice presentations by recording themselves while navigating through PDF slides, then automatically analyzes their performance across multiple dimensions: speech patterns (pacing, pauses, filler words, pitch), content delivery, and slide navigation timing. The system generates personalized, actionable feedback that helps users identify areas for improvement and track their progress across multiple practice attempts.

## Unique Approach

Our system employs a **hybrid AI architecture** that uniquely combines signal processing algorithms with Large Language Model (LLM) orchestration. Unlike systems that rely solely on either algorithmic metrics or AI interpretation, our approach leverages the precision of signal processing for objective measurement while using LLMs (Google Gemini) for contextual understanding and human-readable feedback generation.

The system's uniqueness lies in three key aspects: (1) **Multi-modal integration**—synthesizing audio analysis, speech transcription, and PDF navigation tracking into unified insights; (2) **Two-level intelligence**—providing both immediate attempt-level feedback and session-level trend analysis across multiple practice runs; and (3) **Contextual interpretation**—the AI understands relationships between metrics (e.g., recognizing that high filler word count combined with fast pace indicates nervousness) rather than simply reporting isolated measurements.

This hybrid approach ensures users receive both transparent, verifiable metrics and expert-level coaching advice, addressing Grosz's (2012) central question of how humans and AI systems can think well together by creating a collaborative relationship where AI enhances rather than replaces human expertise.
